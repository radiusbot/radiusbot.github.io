FAQ

Questions, answered

What does RadiusBot build?

Mobile apps for iPhone and Android, and websites. We design, build and launch them, then hand them over to you, with ongoing maintenance if you want it.

Which technologies do you use?

Flutter and React Native. Both let one codebase run on iPhone, Android and the web, so you get every platform without paying for three separate apps.

Do I need to hire or manage developers?

No. We deliver a finished, working product. You tell us what you want, review the designs and test versions, and we take care of the rest.

Will you publish my app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listing, screenshots and privacy details, and take the app through Apple and Google review.

Can my app have subscriptions or in-app purchases?

Yes. We set up subscriptions, free trials and one-time purchases on both stores, like the ones in our own app Lexitile.

What happens after launch?

You own everything: the app or website, its code, and the store and hosting accounts. If you'd like us to keep looking after it, we offer ongoing maintenance: updates, fixes and support for new phone and browser versions.
